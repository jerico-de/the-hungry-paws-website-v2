Welcome to The HungryPaws!

At The HungryPaws PH, we're more than just a grooming salon and pet hotel—we're your partner in keeping your furry family happy. We're dedicated to creating happy tails for every pet, every time!

About Us

The HungryPaws is a one-stop-shop for all your pet needs. We have pet supplies, grooming services, and pet-hotel services for whenever you need someone to care for your pet.

We are dedicated to bringing happiness to your pets by making sure they are well taken care of.

Our Services

Grooming Services

Our grooming team is dedicated to making pets look great and feel even better. Safe, gentle, and joyful—creating happy tails every time!

Full Groom starts at ₱500, while Bath and Blowdry starts at ₱250.

We also offer A La Carte services including nail trim, ear cleaning, teeth cleaning, and more!

Pet Hotel Services

From tiny paws to gentle giants, our pet hotel gives every guest a cozy space to rest, play, and feel loved. Because while you're away, we're here creating happy tails every day!

24 Hours Stay starts at ₱450, while 12 Hours Stay starts at ₱350.

We also offer Day Care services starting at ₱250 for 3 hours then ₱50 for every succeeding hour.
